Full-Service HVAC Care for Aurora Homes and Businesses

Heating and cooling around here means a wide mix of equipment: older gas furnaces on the older streets, newer heat pumps in recent construction, ductless mini-splits in additions and converted garages, and rooftop units on commercial buildings. Our team handles all of it. Our technicians have years in the field, and we know how the weather here, the age of local homes, and local code affect the way equipment runs and fails.

Most homeowners we meet need one of three things: a repair, a replacement, or regular maintenance. Repairs tend to be quick: a burned contactor, a dirty flame sensor, a slow refrigerant leak, or a seized blower motor. Replacements get a proper load calculation, not a guess based on the old unit's tonnage, which keeps temperatures even and stops the new equipment from short-cycling its way to an early failure. Tune-ups include coil cleaning, a refrigerant charge check, safety control testing, and a static pressure reading, which does more than anything else to extend the life of your system.

Commercial work makes up a large share of our work too. We maintain and repair rooftop units, split systems, and ventilation equipment for restaurant kitchens, shops, offices, and multi-unit properties, and we know that a day without cooling hurts the business. To keep that downtime short, we keep common commercial parts stocked, diagnose quickly, and schedule larger jobs around your operating hours whenever possible.

When Your System Fails in Aurora

Call us about a problem and a real person answers, not a phone tree. Common emergencies we handle include air conditioners blowing warm air, outdoor units that hum but won't start, furnaces that ignite and then shut down, breakers that trip every time the system runs, frozen evaporator coils, clogged condensate drains backing up into the furnace cabinet, and thermostats that have stopped responding. Pricing is always upfront, so you know exactly what the work will cost before we begin.

Two situations call for quick action from you before our technician gets there. The first is an iced-over air conditioner: turn the system off at the thermostat and set the fan to "on" so the moving air thaws the coil, because running it frozen can overflow the drain pan and hurt the compressor. The second is a furnace that keeps cycling on and off or produces a burning smell. Turn it off at the service switch and give us a call. That small step can prevent a far bigger repair.

How Aurora Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Change your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age says a lot about what comes next. Most well-maintained air conditioners reach 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that point and also shows higher bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's a strong candidate for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
